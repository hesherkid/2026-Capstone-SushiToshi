### 2026 TEAM NORMS

## Communication & collaboration

Discord is our primary communication channel. Team members will check the group chat at least once per day and respond to messages directed at them within 24 hours. Messages marked urgent should be answered as soon as reasonably possible.

If a team member cannot be reached on Discord, the team will follow up by text message (SMS) and/or email.

Google Drive (Docs), Notion and GitHub are our primary collaboration tools. Notion is the single source of truth for task assignments and soft deadlines.

It is okay to ask the team for help if you cannot finish assigned work by its soft deadline. Asking for help early is always preferred over missing the deadline.

Team members may contact program administration with questions or concerns about the project at any time. However, we will attempt to resolve grievances within the team before involving administration.

## Client communication

Arlyssa McArthur will be the primary client contact. All written correspondence should be proofread by at least one other team member prior to sending. 

All group members, along with the program administrators, must get a carbon copy (CC) of any email messages sent. 

Any verbal communication with the client, such as a phone or video call, must be documented. A written summary will be sent to all team members and program administrators within 24 hours.

## Punctuality & attendance

If you cannot attend a meeting or will be late, tell the team as early as possible. Ideally that is the day before, but no later than 30 minutes before the meeting starts.

An absent member is responsible for catching up, including reading the meeting summary, and for completing their assigned work.

Valid reasons for absence include medical situations, time-sensitive or non-reschedulable appointments, work schedule conflicts, transit or vehicle issues, natural disasters and family emergencies. Invalid excuses include oversleeping, not feeling like it and waiting in line for Starbucks.

Repeatedly failing to notify the team, or repeatedly missing meetings without a valid reason, is grounds for a warning (see Warnings).

## Expectations & deadlines

Every team member is expected to take on a reasonable share of the work. The split will never be exact, but no one should refuse work and still expect credit. Refusing your share of the work is grounds for a warning.

Soft deadlines. Each task has a soft deadline and a possible second soft deadline. Soft deadlines are set when the task is assigned at the Friday meeting. The first soft deadline is at least 2 days before the final deadline, and the second soft deadline, if required, falls before the final deadline. All deadlines are recorded on the Notion board.

Missed or substandard work. Work counts as substandard if it does not meet the task criteria or does not function. If a member misses the first soft deadline, or their work is substandard, they get one chance to fix it by the second soft deadline. If the work is still incomplete or substandard at the second soft deadline, the member receives a warning. 

If you are struggling with a task, tell the team before the deadline so the work can be adjusted or reassigned.

All program student expectations and policies apply to group work, meetings and communication.

## GitHub & development

The main branch is protected. All changes reach main through a pull request that has at least one approval from a team member other than its author.

1. Do all work on a separate branch named by type and purpose, for example feature/login-page or bugfix/null-reference-on-save.
2. Before opening a pull request, make sure the code compiles, works correctly and passes all tests.
3. Open a pull request with a short description of what changed and why, and request a review.
4. The reviewer approves the pull request or requests changes. Reviews should happen within 24 hours of the request.
5. After approval, the pull request's author merges it. Source branches are set to auto delete.

Do not force push to shared branches or make major structural changes without discussing them with the team first.

## Conflict resolution

We will discuss every issue as a team and try to resolve it amicably. 

Technical disagreements should be settled with evidence wherever possible: project requirements, documentation, testing and other technical evidence.

Unresolved disagreements go to a vote. With three members a majority decides (two of three members). If a member abstains and the vote ties, the team will consult program administration.

Small disagreements that do not significantly affect the client or project are settled by a game of team chess between the two sides. If there is no time for chess, a coin flip decides. Before the game or flip, each side states how they will resolve the issue if they win, and the winner resolves it that way.

Severe infractions, such as violating program policies or using hateful language, will be referred to program administration and may lead to removal under the Member removal policy.

## Warnings

A warning is issued only when both other team members agree to it and sign it. Warnings are recorded in a dedicated, version-controlled document.

A warning may be issued when a team member repeatedly fails to meet agreed responsibilities without reasonable communication. Examples include:

- Work still incomplete or substandard at the second soft deadline
- Repeated unexplained absences or failure to notify the team
- Refusing assigned work
- Repeatedly submitting incomplete work

The member receiving a warning will have the opportunity to explain and correct the issue before it is finalized.

If a member accumulates three warnings, the remaining members may request their removal from program administration under the Member removal policy.

Any action that breaks the law or program policies will be referred to program administration or law enforcement.

## Member removal policy

Removal is a last resort, considered only for serious or repeated unresolved issues, such as three recorded warnings or a severe infraction.

To request removal, the remaining members present evidence of the relevant infractions to the instructor or program administration. If the instructor approves, the member is removed from the group, and their status in the project is handled between them and program administration.

## Amendments

Any team member can propose a change to these norms at a team meeting. A change takes effect only when all three members agree, and the updated document is re-signed.
